How the scores work

Value answers one question: is BTC cheap against past cycle lows? Each of 16 signals gets a score from 0 to 100. The score goes up as the signal gets closer to the level it reached at past lows. The value score is the weighted average. If a reading is missing, its weight goes to the others.

Small-cycle levels. Each past low was less deep than the one before. For six signals, the full-score level moves with that trend. Switch to Classic to see the score with the old, fixed levels.

Bottom in? asks a different question: has the low already passed? It looks for signs that only come after a low: no new low for weeks, price back above recent buyers' cost and the 200-day average, selling at a profit again, miners and money coming back. It stays low until the value score has reached 75 at least once in this bear market, because no past low came before that.

Cycle clock shows where this cycle is in time and depth, against the 2015, 2018 and 2022 lows.

Live: price-based signals are worked out again at the live price each time the price updates. The other signals change once a day.
