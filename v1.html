How the score works

Each of 16 signals gets a score from 0 to 100. The score goes up as the signal gets closer to the level it reached at past cycle lows. The bottom score is the weighted average. If a reading is missing, its weight goes to the others.

    Bands: 0–19 Very weak · 20–39 Weak · 40–59 Building · 60–74 Strong · 75–89 Very strong · 90+ Capitulation.

    Live score: nine price-based signals (cost lines, MVRV, 200-week and 200-day averages, Puell, reserve risk) are worked out again at the live price each time the price updates. The other seven change once a day.
