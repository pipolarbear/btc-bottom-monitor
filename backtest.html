8. Out-of-sample test: leave one cycle out

The small-cycle levels are fitted on the 2015, 2018 and 2022 lows. So the score has seen the answer for each of those cycles. This test hides one low at a time: it fits the line on the other two lows, uses that line's level for the hidden cycle, and scores that cycle again. If the results hardly change, the fit is not doing the work.

What this test does not cover: the classic levels and the weights were also chosen with all three lows in view. The classic column shows the score with no trend at all. With three cycles, no test can rule out luck.

10. Limits

  • Three cycles. There are only three past lows to learn from. Neighbouring weeks are not separate tests, so a band with 40 weeks rests on two or three bear markets, not on 40 events.
  • Hindsight. Levels and weights were set after the lows were known. Section 8 tests the part that is fitted by a formula. The rest is judgment and is listed in section 2 so you can change it and see.
  • Weekly readings. The backtest uses Monday readings. A daily backtest finds some extremes a few days earlier or deeper.
  • Data revisions. LookIntoBitcoin and Coin Metrics sometimes revise past values. history.json is a snapshot from November 2026.
  • Not tested. Leverage (funding and open interest) starts in 2023 and money flows in 2024, so they are not in the backtest. Leverage carries 6 of the 100 weight points in the live score; money flows are shown but not scored.
  • Scores, not probabilities. Past lows do not promise future lows. Information only, not financial advice.
